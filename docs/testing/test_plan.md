## 1. Test states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PLANNED` | Procedure and pass criteria defined; not yet executed |
| `NOT RUN` | Was due in a session but deliberately not executed; record why |
| `PASS` | Executed; all acceptance criteria met; evidence recorded |
| `FAIL` | Executed; at least one criterion not met. Keep the record and add a TS entry and a re-test |
| `BLOCKED` | Cannot be executed because of a dependency or environment problem; record the blocker |

A test only becomes PASS or FAIL through an actual execution with recorded output or observation. A re-test after a fix gets a new dated entry (e.g. `TEST-M1-04 (re-test 1)`); the failed entry is kept.

> **Note on the records-consistency checks** (TEST-M2-11, TEST-M3-12, TEST-M4-12): these checkers also compare the records with my private planning files, so their scripts and logs are not published in this repository. Their procedures and results stay listed for completeness.

## 2. Test strategy
1. **Static checks first.** Xacro expansion, URDF structure, and generated-model properties (mass, inertia, geometry) are checked without a GUI. They are fast, repeatable, and run from scripts with captured output.
2. **Visual checks next.** RViz and Gazebo GUI tests are run and observed by me, and recorded as "observed by me". A GUI result is never recorded without someone actually watching it.
3. **Simulation behaviour last.** Spawn and rest stability.
4. **Consistency review.** Documentation, properties, and generated model are compared before a milestone is closed.

## 6. M2 test procedures (criteria Accepted: DEC-027, 2026-10-22)

Plan: [../milestones/M2_basic_movement.md](../milestones/M2_basic_movement.md); requirements: [../requirements.md](../requirements.md) § M2; criteria rationale: [../design/motion_control.md](../design/motion_control.md) §6. Packages per DEC-023; configuration per DEC-024 to DEC-026. Thresholds are not changed to make a failing test pass.

### 6.1 Common conditions (all simulation tests)
- **Environment:** shell in `amr_ws/` after `source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash` and `source install/setup.bash`; `EV=../docs/evidence/M2`.
- **Launch:** `ros2 launch amr_bringup sim.launch.py headless:=true` (stock `empty.sdf` world, 1 kHz physics). Each run uses a fresh launch with a unique `ROS_DOMAIN_ID` and `GZ_PARTITION`, and leftover `gz sim` processes are checked for afterwards (TS-006, TS-009).
- **Initial condition:** model `amr` spawned at the world origin with yaw 0 (`base_footprint` on the ground). Measurement starts once both controllers are active and 2.0 s of simulation time have passed with a zero command.
- **Harness:** `docs/evidence/M2/TEST-M2_motion_measure.py <mode>` (run with plain `python3`, TS-011), started by `bash docs/evidence/M2/TEST-M2_procedure.sh <mode> 3 <log>`. It:
  - takes simulation time from the Gazebo pose-stream stamps (the simulation clock), extrapolated between the ~59 Hz samples, instead of subscribing to the 1 kHz `/clock`; it uses rclpy's single-threaded executor (TS-013);
  - publishes `TwistStamped` commands on `/cmd_vel` at 50 Hz, stamped in simulation time unless the mode says otherwise. Since DEC-029, `/cmd_vel` passes through `cmd_vel_guard` to the controller;
  - records **ground truth**: the `amr` model pose in the world from Gazebo `/world/empty/pose/info` (gz-transport, simulation-time stamped);
  - records **controller outputs**: `/diff_drive_controller/odom`, `/joint_states` and `/diff_drive_controller/cmd_vel_out`.
- **Validity:** RTF = Δsim / Δwall over the measurement window. A run with RTF < 0.5 is rejected and repeated (not a failure).
- **Runs:** 3 valid runs per test; every run must pass. The harness prints each criterion as PASS/FAIL and the measured value.
- **Derived quantities:**
  - *Body-frame displacement:* the ground-truth displacement rotated into the heading at command start (x along, y lateral).
  - *Heading change Δψ and tilt:* from the quaternion (roll, pitch, yaw; fixed-axis X-Y-Z).
  - *Speed:* finite difference of ground-truth position over 0.1 s.
  - *Acceleration:* finite difference of that speed over 0.1 s.
  - *At rest:* ground-truth speed < 5 mm/s, sustained for 0.2 s.
  - *Odometry ratio:* odometry displacement ÷ ground-truth displacement over the same window.
  - *Slip:* 1 − ground-truth distance ÷ (r × mean absolute wheel rotation), from `/joint_states` positions with r = `wheel_radius`.
